## 4. The 15 Minute Call Script

Goal: help them first. Be curious. If it feels natural, open the door to the protection side. If not, that is fine.

Have open before the call: their test result (ask them to share their grade if you do not have it), a notepad, and zainadtani.com/ai-checkup.

### Minute 0 to 1: Warm open

> "Hey [Name], thanks for taking the time. I know you are busy. Here is my plan. I want to look at your test result, talk about missed calls, and see if anything is worth fixing. Sound good?"

### Minute 1 to 4: Their leak

> "What grade did you get?"
>
> "What number did it show for your monthly leak?"
>
> "Does that feel right to you? High? Low?"
>
> "When you miss a call today, what happens? Voicemail? Call back later?"
>
> "When are you most likely to miss calls? On a job? After hours? Weekends?"

Listen. Write down their words. Repeat one back to them: "So when you are on a job, calls just go to voicemail. Got it."

### Minute 4 to 7: The AI checkup

> "Here is how I help. I do a free AI checkup. I look at how calls and messages come in, and where they slip through. Then I show you simple fixes."
>
> "If you want me to set it up and run it with you, I have a partner plan. It is $250 a month. No pressure on that today. The checkup alone is free and useful."
>
> "Want me to book the checkup for you now?"

If yes, book it before moving on. If they want to think, say: "Totally fine. I will text you the link." Then send zainadtani.com/ai-checkup.

### Minute 7 to 8: The soft bridge

Only use this if the call feels warm. Read the room.

> "One more thing on your result. The test also gave you a Stopped Tomorrow Score. Did you look at that part?"
>
> "A lot of owners skip it. It is the one I think about most, because I am also a licensed life and health agent here in Texas. Can I ask you a couple questions about it? If not, no worries at all."

If they say no, thank them and go to the close. Do not push.

### Minute 8 to 13: The protection side (educational only)

Ask. Do not tell.

> "If you got hurt and could not work for a few months, what would happen to the business?"
>
> "Would the bills at home still get paid? How long could that go?"
>
> "If something happened to you, does your family have a plan? Have you talked about it with anyone?"
>
> "Is there someone who could keep the business going without you?"

Then share what the score means in plain words:

> "Your score is just a rough look at how long things keep running if you stop. It is not advice. It is a starting point for a real talk."
>
> "Some owners like to sit down and map out what would happen in each case. I do a free 15 minute protection review for that. It is just a conversation. You leave with a clearer picture, and you decide what, if anything, to do."

Things you never say on this call: product names, prices or premiums, "you need this," "this is guaranteed," or anything that sounds like a deadline.

### Minute 13 to 15: Close

> "So, to recap. Step one is your free AI checkup. Want to lock that in?"
>
> "And if you want the protection review later, here is my calendar: calendly.com/zkadtani. No rush."
>
> "Thanks again, [Name]. I will text you a short recap today."

After the call, send the recap text within one hour:

> "Great talking, [Name]. Your AI checkup: zainadtani.com/ai-checkup. If you ever want the free protection review: calendly.com/zkadtani. Here if you have questions. Z"
